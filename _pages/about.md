Hello! I'm Kasra Torshizi, a second-year Computer Science PhD student at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by [Dr. Pratap Tokekar](http://tokekar.com/) in the [Robotics Algorithms and Autonomous Systems (RAAS) Lab](http://raaslab.org/). My research is in robotics and machine learning, with a focus on planning and control. A central theme of my work is ensuring that robot actions stay aligned with what we intend. My most recent work, [STeP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18580), does this by using temporal logic as a bridge between natural language and low-level control, so that spatial, logical, and temporal constraints are enforced and monitored during execution. I've also worked on making learned estimators and controllers adapt robustly to changing conditions with little data ([Contextual-NeuroMHE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24281)), and on risk-aware decision making that keeps the probability of failure bounded ([RiskRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02788)).

This past summer I was a reinforcement learning intern at [Systems and Technology Research](https://str.us/), where my work focused on strategy alignment in multi-agent reinforcement learning, including methods that keep a learned policy close to an imitation-learned strategy while it optimizes additional reward objectives.

I also work closely with [Dr. Donald Costello](https://eng.umd.edu/clark/faculty/1903/Donald-Bucket-Costello-III) in the [Maryland Autonomous Technologies Research Innovation and eXploration (MATRIX) Lab](https://matrix.umd.edu/), where I develop control loops for autonomous UAV landings on Navy vessels in turbulent and low-visibility conditions. I completed my bachelors (with a minor in Mathematics) and masters degrees in Computer Science at UMD. In my free time, I enjoy skiing, hiking, and cooking.
